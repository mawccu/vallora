03 Ethos

NOT JUST CLOTHING.
A MINDSET.

Three ideas, and everything the brand does comes back to one of them. They are the reason the catalogue is short and the reason the print is on the back rather than the front.

01

Fearless soul

The print is a reminder, not a slogan. It sits on your back because it is the part of you other people see when you keep going. Nobody buys a shirt to be told who they are, so the front stays quiet and the back does the talking, and only to the people behind you.

02

Worldwide mobility

Made to travel. Light enough for heat, heavy enough to hold its shape after the hundredth wash. The cut is oversized because clothes that are built to move have to have somewhere to move to, and everything is chosen so it still looks right after a day that did not go to plan.

03

Released in phases

No endless catalogue. Small, deliberate drops, each one closed before the next begins. Four pieces at a time is enough to say something and few enough that every one of them earns its place. When a phase closes it stays closed: no restocks, no quiet reprints.

Based

Amman, Jordan. Everything is shot on location here, which is why the light in the lookbook is what it is.

Made

Heavy cotton, oversized cut, printed locally. Ordered by DM, delivered by hand or by post.

Since

2026. Phase 01 is the first, and it is live now.
