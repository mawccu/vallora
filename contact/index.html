Before you ask

How do I order?
Pick your piece and size on its page, then tap Order on WhatsApp. The message writes itself with the piece and the size in it, so you only have to hit send. Instagram DM works exactly as well if that is where you live.
What size should I take?
Everything is cut heavy and oversized, so most people take their normal size. Send us your usual size and your height and we will tell you which one to take. Measurements are on each piece's page.
Why is there no checkout?
Because the run is small and every order is confirmed by a person. Pricing and availability are given over DM until checkout opens.
Will Phase 01 come back?
No. Each phase is closed before the next begins, and closed means closed. No restocks and no quiet reprints.
I own a piece. Can I leave a review?
Yes, and we would rather have yours than write our own. Every review on this site is a real customer's words. Send your photo and what you think and it goes up on that piece's page.
